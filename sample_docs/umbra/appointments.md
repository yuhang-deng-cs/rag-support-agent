# Appointment Scheduling

Umbra Health lets clinics publish online booking pages for each practitioner.
Patients can book, reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before the appointment; later changes must be made by phone.

## Reminders

Patients receive an SMS reminder 48 hours before the appointment and an email reminder 24 hours before.
Clinics can disable SMS reminders in Settings > Notifications to avoid SMS charges.

## No-shows

A no-show fee can be configured per practitioner. It is charged automatically to the card on file 1 hour after a missed appointment.
