# Data Retention and Deletion

Acme Cloud stores customer data in the region chosen at sign-up: US (Virginia) or EU (Frankfurt).
Data never leaves the selected region, including backups.

Deleted projects stay in the trash for 30 days and can be restored by any workspace admin.
After 30 days they are permanently deleted. Encrypted backups are kept for a further 35 days and then destroyed.

When a workspace cancels its subscription, all data is retained for 90 days so it can be exported, and is then permanently deleted.
Admins can request immediate deletion by contacting support; deletion is confirmed in writing within 7 days.
