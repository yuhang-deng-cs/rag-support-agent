# Billing and Refunds

Acme Cloud bills every workspace monthly in advance, on the same calendar day the subscription started.
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and can be downloaded from Settings > Billing.

## Plans

The Starter plan costs $29 per month and includes 3 seats.
The Team plan costs $99 per month and includes 15 seats; extra seats cost $8 per seat per month.
Enterprise pricing is negotiated annually and includes SSO, audit logs and a 99.95% uptime SLA.

## Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase.
Monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period.
Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails we retry three times over 7 days.
After the final retry the workspace becomes read-only until the outstanding invoice is paid.
